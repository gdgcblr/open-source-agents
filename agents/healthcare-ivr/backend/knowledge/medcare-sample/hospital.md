## About the hospital

MedCare General Hospital is a 200-bed multi-specialty hospital in Bangalore, established in 2005. It has twelve departments, more than fifty doctors, a 24-hour emergency department, and in-house pharmacy, laboratory and imaging services.

## Location and contact

- **Address:** 42 Rajaji Nagar Main Road, Bangalore, Karnataka 560010. The hospital is close to the metro station.
- **Main reception:** 080 4455 6789
- **Emergency department:** 080 4455 0000 (24 hours)
- **Ambulance:** 080 4455 1111 (24 hours, with basic and advanced life support)
- **Patient relations:** extension 1200
- **Parking:** free basement parking for patients and visitors, with space for 150 vehicles

## Appointments

- Book by calling this number, calling the main reception, or visiting the hospital. Walk-in patients are seen when a doctor is free.
- The receptionist on this line books appointments directly once the caller agrees to the doctor, date and time, and gives a booking reference.
- Cancellations are free up to four hours before the appointment. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged ₹200.
- For a first visit, bring a photo ID, your insurance card, any previous medical records or test reports, and a list of the medicines you take.

## Insurance and payments

- **Insurance:** Star Health, HDFC Ergo, ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz, New India Assurance, and United India Insurance. Bring your insurance card and a valid ID for cashless treatment.
- **Payment methods:** cash, credit and debit cards, UPI, and net banking. EMI options are available for procedures above ₹20,000.
- **Health checkup packages:** from ₹1,500, including basic screening, executive, cardiac, and women's health checkups. Reception can share details.

## Other services

- **Pharmacy:** in-house, stocking all commonly prescribed medicines (see Timings).
- **Medical records:** request copies at the medical records department on the first floor. Copies are usually ready within two working days, for a small fee.
- **Cafeteria:** ground floor, with vegetarian and non-vegetarian food. Special diet meals are served to patients as prescribed by their doctor.
- **Feedback and complaints:** use the feedback forms at reception, or call patient relations on extension 1200.

## Patient rights

Every patient may receive information about their diagnosis and treatment options, ask for a second opinion, and refuse treatment. Patient information is kept strictly confidential.
