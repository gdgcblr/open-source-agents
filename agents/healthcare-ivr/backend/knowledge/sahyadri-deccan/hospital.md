## About the hospital

Sahyadri Super Speciality Hospital at Deccan Gymkhana is a 225-bed multispeciality hospital in Pune. It belongs to Sahyadri Hospitals, an NABH-accredited group that describes itself as the largest chain of hospitals in Maharashtra. The group began in 1994 as the Pune Institute of Neurology, a 16-bed centre that grew into Maharashtra's first dedicated neurology and neurosurgery centre, and opened Sahyadri Speciality Hospital in November 2004.
