      How it works

      Built with Google ADK and Gemini.

      1. 1

        Instant check on your phone

        Built-in rules check the message right away, even with no internet.

      2. 2

        Three AI investigators, in parallel

        • Links: fake, short and look-alike websites
        • Payments: requests for OTP, UPI PIN, fees or QR scans
        • Scam patterns: KYC, parcel, job, digital arrest and more
      3. 3

        A verdict agent gives a risk score, the warning signs and what to do, in your language.

      Privacy and safety

      Messages are not stored

      Each check is deleted when it ends. For the AI check, the message is sent to Google Gemini.

      No trackers

      No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party scripts.

      Banks never ask for OTP or PIN

      Anyone who asks for your OTP, UPI PIN or password is a scammer. Never share them.
