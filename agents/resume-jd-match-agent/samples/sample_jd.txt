Senior Product Manager - Payments Platform
FinFlow (fictional company) | Bengaluru (Hybrid)

About the role
FinFlow helps mid-market businesses collect payments faster. We are hiring a Senior
Product Manager to own our Payments Platform: the APIs and dashboards that thousands of
merchants use to accept and reconcile payments. You will work closely with engineering,
design, data and go-to-market teams.

What you will do
- Own the product strategy and roadmap for the Payments Platform.
- Run product discovery with merchants and turn insights into clear PRDs and user stories.
- Define KPIs and OKRs for the platform and report progress to leadership.
- Design and analyse A/B tests to improve conversion across the checkout funnel.
- Partner with engineering on API design for our developer-facing payment APIs.
- Lead cross-functional collaboration with sales, support and marketing on feature launches.
- Mentor two Associate Product Managers.

Must-have qualifications
- 5+ years of total experience, including 3+ years in product management.
- Experience shipping B2B SaaS or payments products.
- Strong data analysis skills: comfortable writing SQL and working in Mixpanel or Amplitude.
- Hands-on experience with A/B testing and experimentation.
- Experience defining KPIs and OKRs.
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
- Working knowledge of REST APIs and API products.

Nice-to-have
- Technical background (former engineer) or a Computer Science degree.
- Experience with Stripe, Razorpay or similar payment gateways.
- Familiarity with Looker or Tableau.
- Exposure to Generative AI or LLM features in fintech products.
- Experience with pricing and monetization strategy.

What we offer
Competitive salary, ESOPs, health insurance, and a hybrid work setup.
