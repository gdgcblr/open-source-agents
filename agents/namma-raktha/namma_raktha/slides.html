Context & Motivation

The Bengaluru Golden Hour Crisis

Urban Healthcare Challenge
01. Bengaluru Urban Gridlock

Severe transit chokepoints (ORR, Silk Board, Hebbal, Dairy Circle) create 60–90 minute delays during critical golden-hour medical emergencies.

02. Seasonal Dengue Platelet Shortage

Platelet (SDP) apheresis demands rigorous pre-checks (48-72h NSAID clearance, minimum platelet count). Volunteers travel across the city only to be clinically disqualified.

03. Unverified WhatsApp Spam Chaos

Emergency forwards circulate without verified hospital IP numbers, specific blood component criteria, or valid attendant phone numbers, overwhelming blood banks.

04. Illegal Paid Blood Commercial Scams

Fraudulent actors exploit panicked families in ICU corridors with illegal commercial blood trading and UPI scams, violating Supreme Court and National Blood Policy mandates.

Objective: Transform frantic unverified text into a verified, clinically-routed, anti-scam dispatch within seconds.

Clinical Rigor & Safety

Clinical Compliance & Safety Defense

NBTC India Aligned
Rare Bombay (hh) Phenotype Protocols

Detects Bombay blood group anti-H antibodies. Strictly blocks regular O-negative and instantly triggers alerts for Sankalp India Foundation and Rotary TTK rare blood registries.

Apheresis & Platelet (SDP) Restrictions

Enforces the mandatory 48-72h NSAID (Aspirin/Disprin/Ibuprofen) exclusion, >50kg weight limit, and arm vein checks before dispatching donors to dengue wards.

Anti-Commercialization Scam Block

Enforces National Blood Policy and Supreme Court directives. Rejects paid blood buying/selling, monetary bribes, or UPI demands to protect voluntary non-remunerated donation.

Deterministic Offline Resilience

When network drops occur in emergency hospital basements, the agent falls back to deterministic regex clinical matching, ensuring 100% operational availability without API keys.

Safety Summary: Zero hallucinations on clinical incompatibility. Anti-scam protection active at the prompt layer.
