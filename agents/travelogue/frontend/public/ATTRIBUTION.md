# Bundled assets

- `world.json`: simplified properties from Natural Earth's `ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson`, obtained from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector. Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Generalized geometry is not an authoritative border or exhaustive territory list.
- `geography.json`: Natural Earth's 1:110m lakes, river centerlines, and country-label coordinates from the same public-domain source. These are generalized geographic context, not street-level or navigation data.
- `art/*.svg`: original code-generated illustrations, released under the repository's MIT license. They are artistic representations, not photographs of the named locations. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/generate-art.py`.
- Sample journal stories are fictional workshop fixtures. Tourism URLs are reference links, not verified support for every seasonal claim.
