## 🌟 The Core Innovation: Financial Invariants vs. LLM Hallucinations

In traditional AI applications, LLMs are asked to calculate numbers directly in natural language. This invariably causes **floating-point drift**, **lost pennies**, and **hallucinated price levels**.

**OmniLedger AI** inverts this pattern:
1. **Deterministic Core:** All mathematical calculations, double-entry conservation laws, Camarilla equilibrium bounds, and regulatory concentration caps are executed by audited, zero-token deterministic Python engines in $<1.5\text{ms}$.
2. **AI Synthesis Layer:** Google Gemini 2.5 Flash and Google Gemma-2-9B are used exclusively for what LLMs do best: **multimodal receipt vision**, **unstructured natural language parsing**, and **executive strategic thesis synthesis**.
3. **Google ADK Hub-and-Spoke Pattern:** Both systems run separate, highly specialized Google ADK hubs (`urn:adk:hub:bharat-dues-master` and `urn:adk:hub:arthaniti-quant-master`), ensuring clean domain boundaries.
