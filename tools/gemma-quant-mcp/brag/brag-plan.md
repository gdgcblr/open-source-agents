# Brag Plan: Arthaniti
**Title**: Arthaniti: Institutional Rigor
**Tone**: Cinematic & Authoritative

"Precision Over Hallucinations." Contrast the unpredictability of standard AI with the "Guardrail" certainty of Arthaniti.
